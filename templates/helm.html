Helm × HVL Family Office: Two Complementary Takes on the Household Command Center

Looking at both architectures side by side, it is striking how little wasted overlap there is in our core philosophies. While both platforms are multi-tenant web applications with interactive public demo environments and daily institutional sync via SimpleFIN, each started from an opposite end of the family office problem — making the two approaches genuinely complementary.

🧭 Helm's Core Lens: Present-Tense Balance Sheet, LP Book & Continuity

Helm treats the household balance sheet as an observational, present-day system of record and household continuity ("Green Box"). It avoids speculative projections: money is integer cents, staleness is surfaced explicitly, private fund umbrellas are only as fresh as their oldest mark, tax lots know their holding period to the day, and the UI adapts between the CFO seat, the COO seat, and age-gated Kid views so either spouse can run the household in a crisis.

📈 HVL's Core Lens: Multi-Plan Scenario Forecasting & Side-by-Side Comparison

HVL Family Office treats the household balance sheet as the launchpad for multi-plan 30-year scenario forecasting and side-by-side plan comparison. Families compose competing life plans — dragging in liquidity events, sabbatical/retirement dates, real estate moves, 529 superfunding, Roth conversion ladders, and PE capital calls — and compare their trajectories head-to-head across vectorized Monte Carlo trials and a 50-state statutory tax & equity engine.
