Section 1 • Engineering Summary (Post-v0.5.3 → v0.5.4)

Verified Engineering Deliverables (v{{ app_version | default('0.5.4') }})

6 Workstreams

Workstream A: Multi-Strategy Company Equity Exercise Engine & Staged Multi-Year Tranches (forecast/waterfall_equity.py, modals_plan_edit.js)

Problem: Company equity life events previously treated option exercises as single-year cashless liquidations and lacked per-scenario custom equity appreciation overrides, retained share valuation, or post-exercise LTCG share sales.
Fix: Extracted forecast/waterfall_equity.py supporting 4 holding, exercise & exit strategies (cashless_repurchase, exercise_and_hold_cash, exercise_and_hold_sell_to_cover, and sell_held_shares taxed at LTCG + NIIT + WA State Cap Gains above the stepped-up cost basis), staged multi-year tranches (start_year to end_year), post-tax Net Worth inclusion of retained company shares and unexercised vested options, custom per-scenario Equity Growth Rate (%) overrides in Edit Plan, and automatic company equity liquidation fallback to cover unfunded retirement deficits.

Workstream B: 23-Tool AI Advisor (update_scenario_assumptions), Copy-on-Write Event Isolation & Multimodal Chat (tools_scenarios_equity.py, scenario_clone_service.py)

Problem: Editing a life event shared across multiple scenarios mutated sibling scenarios in-place, and the AI Advisor lacked a dedicated tool to update, branch, or prune scenario-level retirement/spending/equity growth assumptions.
Fix: Added update_scenario_assumptions with delete_all_child_scenarios support (bringing the suite to 23 deterministic tools with Before vs. After net worth deltas at ages 65/85/95), exposed company_equity in get_annual_cash_flow_detail and scenario_name on get_monte_carlo_stress_test, implemented automatic copy-on-write cloning when modifying shared life events, added a collapsible SCENARIO EVENTS (N) strip with normalized custom SVG icons, and enabled multimodal file/image attachments in the AI Advisor drawer.

Workstream C: Notification Center, Computed Health Alerts, Lock-Screen Web Push & Siri Voice Webhook (routes/notifications.py, routes/siri.py, _device_integrations.html)

Problem: Background sync outcomes and household health warnings were not surfaced in a persistent header notification center or on mobile lock screens/voice assistants.
Fix: Added the header Notification Bell and slide-out drawer combining asynchronous job status alerts with computed household health checks (stale institutional syncs, quarterly Safe Harbor shortfalls, unreviewed readiness items), optional VAPID lock-screen Web Push notifications, an installable PWA manifest/service worker (sw.js), and a token-wrapped Siri & Apple Shortcuts voice webhook (POST /api/siri with hvl_siri_... keys).

Workstream D: Pure-Python Static Asset Bundler, Embedded Initial SSR State & 12h Monarch Cooldown (bundle_config.json, static/vendor/bundle-*.min.js, monarch/client_graphql.py)

Problem: Loading dozens of individual CSS/JS modules and firing a separate initial /api/dashboard XHR after SSR added unnecessary round-trips, and rapid manual Monarch sync clicks could re-query upstream GraphQL.
Fix: Built hvl_common.ui.bundler to emit content-hashed CSS/JS bundles in static/vendor/, embedded sanitized #fo-initial-dashboard-data JSON into the initial SSR HTML payload to eliminate the duplicate bootstrap XHR, and enforced a 12-hour default cooldown on manual Monarch syncs unless explicit force-refresh is requested.

Workstream E: Legacy family_office.db Retirement, SQLAlchemy Operational Models & Cold-Eyes Transcript Gate (db/models_ops.py, scripts/export_public_repo.py)

Problem: Legacy flat-file migration shims for family_office.db remained in db/migrations.py, operational tables (notifications, push_subscriptions, siri_tokens) needed declarative ORM models, and public repo exports needed cryptographic proof that a subagent ran the Cold-Eyes audit.
Fix: Retired legacy family_office.db splitting code in favor of the Database-per-Family architecture (data/{universe}/families/{id}.db), added SQLAlchemy operational models in db/models_ops.py, and enforced transcript verification of --cold-eyes-verified <tree_hash> in scripts/export_public_repo.py.

Workstream F: Scenario Archiving, Drag-and-Drop Plan Reordering & Mobile Geometry (services/scenario_archive_service.py, services/scenario_reorder_service.py, cards_reorder.js, scorecard.html)

Problem: Households with numerous scenario branches could not archive obsolete plans without permanent deletion, could not customize comparison row order, and 375px mobile viewports experienced horizontal overflow from long Siri token URLs and child plan selector badges.
Fix: Implemented non-destructive scenario archiving with #btn-toggle-archived-scenarios toggle (Show/Hide Archived (N)), table/card Archive buttons, and baseline/active plan protection; added two-way HTML5 drag-and-drop plan reordering with immediate canvas trajectory re-renders and POST /api/scenarios/reorder persistence; and resolved 375px mobile overflow in Siri token displays and child plan badges.
