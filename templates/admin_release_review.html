Tracks the incremental changes accumulated since the v0.4.1 release baseline across concurrent workspace sessions before exporting v0.5.0 to the public repository.

Section 1 • Post-v0.4.1 Incremental Delta

Engineering Summary Since v0.4.1 (What Changed & Why)

4 Feature Workstreams in v0.5.0

Workstream A: Unbundled Forecasting Canvas, 4 Overview Graph Widgets & Collapsible Domain Charts (ssr/widgets_charts.py, static/js/scenarios/charts/)

Problem: The single-view canvas_lenses.html tab bar forced users to flip back and forth between Net Worth, Cash Flow/Sankey, Monte Carlo, and Longevity instead of viewing or ordering them together, and Overview lacked opt-in projection graphs.
Fix: Retired canvas_lenses.html and unbundled Forecasting charts into standalone, reorderable widget cards (Lifetime Net Worth Trajectory locked as Core alongside companion Annual Cash Flow & Tax Drawdown with Timeline Bars | Single-Year Sankey toggle, Monte Carlo Wealth Fan P10–P90, and Actuarial Longevity); added 4 opt-in Overview graph widgets in ssr/widgets_charts.py and static/js/widget_charts.js; added persistent collapsible domain chart toggles across Holdings, Equity, Compensation, and Philanthropy; and persisted widget/chart layout state for Demo/Viewer sessions.

Workstream B: 19-Tool AI Advisor (Scenario Events, LP Book, Tax Checklist) & Dynamic Bracket-Fill Roth Conversions (tools_scenarios.py, tools_operations.py)

Problem: The AI Financial Advisor could only inspect the active scenario and lacked operational tools to manage scenario life events, private LP commitments, or tax season document statuses; and Roth conversion events required static annual amounts rather than dynamically filling statutory Federal tax brackets.
Fix: Expanded the AI Advisor from 14 to 19 deterministic Python tools—adding manage_scenario_life_event (with multi-scenario lookup by name and Before vs. After net worth/FI crossover deltas), get_private_markets_lp_book, manage_lp_commitment, update_tax_document_checklist_status, and audit_household_risks; and upgraded Roth conversion events to support dynamic annual ceiling presets (auto_bracket_24, auto_bracket_22, and rule_72t_sepp).

Workstream C: Survivorship Readiness Audit (✓ Mark Verified), Private LP Commitment Book & Per-Plan Drawdown Override (household_audit_service.py, private_markets_service.py)

Problem: Households lacked a deterministic survivorship/probate readiness scorecard with custodian verification, private equity/venture LP commitments had no unfunded capital call stress-testing or multiple tracking (DPI/RVPI/TVPI), and withdrawal sequencing was fixed across plans.
Fix: Built the 8-rule deterministic Survivorship & Household Readiness Audit (0–100 score on Estate + optional Overview widget + deep-link action buttons + persistent ✓ Mark Verified custodian sign-off via POST /api/estate/audit_verify); built the Private Investment / LP Commitment Book with DPI, RVPI, TVPI, *"Oldest Mark Wins"* staleness tracking, and a per-plan 18-month capital call stress toggle; and added per-plan Drawdown Order overrides (Taxable → Pre-Tax → Roth, Pre-Tax First (Bracket Fill), Pro-Rata Blend) in forecast/waterfall.py.

Workstream D: Tax Season Document Checklist, Monarch Core Burn Calibrator & Peer DB Sync Hardening (services/tax_checklist_service.py, db/session.py)

Problem: Households tracked annual tax forms manually outside the app, Monarch cash flows needed 1-click steady-state living burn calibration excluding mortgage P&I and child expenses, and INSERT OR REPLACE in peer auth sync risked triggering foreign-key cascade deletes.
Fix: Built services/tax_checklist_service.py to auto-derive expected annual tax forms (W-2, 1099, 3921, K-1, 1099-Q, 1098, 8283) with Waiting → Received → Sent to CPA status pills; added Monarch Core Burn auto-calibration and sync freshness badges on Accounts; and replaced INSERT OR REPLACE with ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE in _sync_auth_records_to_peer_db plus global auth self-healing in db/users.py.
