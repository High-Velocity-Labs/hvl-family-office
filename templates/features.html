A personal tool built to model household financial scenarios beyond standard budget apps and spreadsheets—including branching multi-decade scenario trees, private company stock options, state estate tax cliffs, and an AI assistant connected to calculation engines.

Household Financial Stack

How This Fits With Account Trackers & Spreadsheets

Most households manage finances with a combination of account aggregators and custom spreadsheets. Here is how the workflow divides, and what this tool automates versus what spreadsheets or aggregators already handle well.

Workflow Roles • Where Each Tool Shines
Day-to-Day Ledger

Bank Trackers & Aggregators

Best for: Live bank sync via SimpleFIN, Monarch, or Empower; categorizing monthly credit card expenses, merchant tracking, and monitoring current cash balances.

Boundaries: Day-to-day transaction ledger. Does not model future tax brackets, branching life decisions, or multi-decade projections.
Ad-Hoc Scratchpad

Excel & Google Sheets

Best for: Completely custom math, unique household calculations, and quick ad-hoc modeling that doesn't fit standard software.

Boundaries: Formulas must be maintained manually across tax years. Branching scenarios requires duplicating sheets, which can easily desync.
Scenario Modeling

HVL Family Office

Best for: Ingesting balances from SimpleFIN, Monarch, or CSV, comparing multiple "what-if" scenarios side-by-side, calculating state estate tax cliffs, and running Monte Carlo risk tests.

Boundaries: Built for multi-decade forecasting and statutory tax calculations; not designed for daily merchant receipt tagging.
