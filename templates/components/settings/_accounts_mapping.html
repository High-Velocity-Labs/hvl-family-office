Account Role Classifications

Classify connected accounts into specific Family Office domain categories.

Why Classifications Matter
  • True Net Worth Accuracy: Custodial education accounts (529 Plans and 530A Coverdell ESAs) are legally owned by children and are properly excluded from parents' personal liquid net worth.
  • Philanthropy / DAF: Donor-Advised Funds are charitable assets separate from household liquid reserves.
  • Education Tracking: Assigning 529 and 530A accounts to specific children links them directly to projected university tuition liabilities.
  • Hide from Family Office: Exclude manual stock options, tax offsets, or duplicate accounts from the Family Office balance sheet & investments while preserving aggregator-reported net worth.
Status: Confirmed vs. Suggested

Suggested: Automatically inferred from account names by the AI during account sync.
Confirmed: Locked by you. Confirmed accounts are permanently protected against being overwritten during future automated account syncs.
