Local Desktop Vault — Self-Service Reference Full Documentation & Guides ↗
  • Where Your Data Lives: Your encrypted SQLite vault is stored locally on your machine (macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/HVLFamilyOffice · Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\HVLFamilyOffice). Click Open Data Folder above to view it in Finder/Explorer.
  • Recovery Key (Forgot PIN): Click Copy Recovery Key above and save the 44-character token in your password manager (also stored on disk at .mm/.secret.key). You need this key (or Google Sign-In) if you ever forget your PIN.
  • Backups & Moving Computers: Click Backup (.fovault) to download a portable single-file snapshot of your household, and Restore (.fovault) on any machine to import it. App upgrades also save automatic backups/pre_upgrade_*.fovault snapshots before migrating.
  • Connecting Accounts & AI: Use the Data Integrations & AI Key tab in Settings (or click Guided Setup in the top bar) to connect SimpleFIN Bridge (direct bank sync), Monarch Money, W-2/IRS Transcript PDFs, or your free Google AI Studio Gemini key.
