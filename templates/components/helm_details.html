What Stands Out in Helm (Ideas HVL Can Learn From)

Where Helm's product design and domain modeling really shine for Long Angle households.

Helm's Standout Ideas

1. "Oldest Mark Wins" LP & K-1 Book

Modeling private fund programs as line-item funds with independent statement dates — where re-marking one fund cannot make the other five look fresh — plus straight-lining unfunded capital calls against liquid cash and auto-seeding annual K-1 trackers is spot-on for LP-heavy portfolios.

2. Probate & Titling Transfer Map

Deriving what happens at death directly from each account's titling and beneficiary field (and immediately calling out individually titled accounts without a TOD or retirement accounts missing a beneficiary that would stall in probate) is much more actionable day-to-day than pure estate-tax math.

3. The "Needs Attention" Survivorship Rules

Deriving operational risks automatically from the data — especially catching essential insurance bills autopaying from a credit card that would close upon one spouse's death, or flagging former household contractors who still hold a keypad code — is brilliant practical engineering.

4. CFO vs. COO vs. Kid Role Lenses

Recognizing that most households have a financial spouse (CFO) and an operational spouse (COO) who need different default home screens over the exact same underlying database — plus a calm, non-intimidating emergency Playbook — solves the biggest adoption hurdle in family finance software.

5. Honest Lot Coverage & 60-Day Horizon

Refusing to estimate cost basis when broker exports are partial, explicitly reporting the percentage of positions with known basis, and highlighting short-term lots that will cross the 1-year long-term threshold within 60 days.

6. Personal Securities Compliance Export

Generating employer compliance filings (Account List, Reportable Holdings, and an audit-trail Working Paper that explains why each ETF/mutual fund was dropped as exempt while keeping individual stocks and private placements) from the existing ledger.

What HVL Family Office Focused On (Ideas Helm Might Find Interesting)

Where HVL went deep on multi-plan scenario comparison, statutory tax law, simulation, and tool-calling AI.

HVL's Core Differentiators

1. Multi-Plan Scenario Shelf & Comparison

Instead of a single static projection, families maintain multiple named life plans side-by-side (e.g. Baseline vs. Sabbatical + Secondary Home vs. Early FIRE + Roth Ladder) and overlay their net worth, liquidity, and drawdown trajectories on the same chart.

2. Visual Event Timeline & Year Scrubber

An interactive event shelf dynamically synthesizes draggable timeline pins from the household's real data (option exercises, K–12 & college waterfalls, real estate purchases, state relocations, PE capital calls) with a year-by-year scrubber ribbon inspecting every pool and tax line.

3. Vectorized 3-Pool Monte Carlo Simulator

Simulates multi-decade trajectories across Taxable, Pre-Tax, and Roth buckets with dynamic withdrawal ordering, automated Roth conversion ladders during low-bracket retirement years, age-65 Medicare IRMAA surcharges, RMDs, and dynamic spending guardrails.

4. Federal + 50-State Statutory Tax & Equity Engine

Computes progressive federal/state brackets, FICA, 3.8% NIIT, ISO AMT crossover, and RSU/ISO/NSO refresh grant withholding gaps — plus in-memory PDF extraction for IRS Tax Return/Wage Transcripts and Form W-2s.

5. Tool-Calling AI Advisor Over Live Engines

Rather than a generic chat wrapper, the integrated AI Advisor invokes deterministic Python simulation engines on demand — running Monte Carlo stress tests, option exercise tax math, and state-relocation arbitrage while stripping PII before calling Gemini.

6. Envelope-Encrypted Vault Cloud Escrow & Live Sandbox

Sensitive SQLite columns are encrypted with a per-household Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) key stored in the user's private Google Drive appDataFolder (leaving the server key physically NULL when Support Lock is active), paired with a 1-click editable no-login /sandbox.
