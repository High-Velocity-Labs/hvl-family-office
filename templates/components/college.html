SECURE 2.0 § 126 Rollover & Legacy Strategy

When 529 education custodial accounts are fully funded, up to $35,000 of leftover 529 funds per beneficiary can be rolled over completely tax-free and penalty-free into their personal Roth IRAs under IRC § 529(c)(3)(E) (SECURE 2.0 Act § 126). Remaining funds can pay for graduate/medical/law schools, or be transferred to future family beneficiaries with zero tax consequences.
