6. Desktop Installers, Local PIN Recovery & Self-Hosting macOS · Windows · Docker · Source

Instructions for running HVL Family Office 100% offline via the standalone desktop installers, managing your local vault files and PIN, or deploying via Docker/source.

macOS (.dmg) & Windows (.exe) Installers

  • macOS: Download HVL-Family-Office-macOS.dmg from GitHub Releases and drag HVL Family Office.app to /Applications. If macOS Gatekeeper prompts on first launch, right-click the app and select Open, or run: xattr -cr "/Applications/HVL Family Office.app".
  • Windows: Download and run HVL-Family-Office-Windows-Setup.exe from GitHub Releases (installs per-user into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\HVLFamilyOffice; no Admin rights required).
  • Zero-Data-Loss Updates: Installing a newer .dmg or .exe never overwrites your vault data; on launch, it automatically backs up backups/pre_upgrade_*.fovault and migrates your schema in-place.

Where Local Desktop Vault Files Live on Disk

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/HVLFamilyOffice/
  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\HVLFamilyOffice\
  • Linux / Docker: ~/.local/share/hvl-family-office/ (or /app/data)
  • Contains global.db, families/1.db, .mm/.secret.key (44-char local recovery key), and backups/. Click Open Data Folder in Settings to view in Finder/Explorer.

Local Desktop PIN & Recovery Key

  • First-Run PIN: On first desktop launch, create a local PIN or Passphrase (4–128 chars, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 600,000 iterations) and copy your 44-character Recovery Key in Settings → Household & Access.
  • Forgotten PIN Reset: Click "Forgot PIN or Passphrase?" on /login and paste the 44-character key from .mm/.secret.key inside your local vault folder (or sign in with Google if linked).

Building from Source & Docker

Compile your own native desktop installer or run via Docker:

pip install -r requirements.txt pyinstaller python scripts/build_desktop.py

Outputs are written to dist/HVL-Family-Office-macOS.dmg or dist/HVL-Family-Office-Windows-Setup.exe.

7. Financial Modules & Methodology Reference All 11 Platform Engines

Inside the app, you can click "How It Works" in the section header on any page to open the contextual slide-out drawer for that module. Below is the complete reference across all 11 modules:

Forecasting, Scenario Trees & Forecast CSV Export

Simulates 50-year liquid net worth, cash flows, and retirement readiness (2026–2076). Create a Baseline Parent Plan and branch lightweight Child Plans (e.g., early retirement, state relocation, private vs. public college) that inherit parent accounts while isolating decision overrides. Organize comparison plans using two-way HTML5 drag-and-drop reordering across selector pills and scorecard rows, and archive obsolete plans without deleting them via the Show / Hide Archived toggle. Includes a 1,000+ trial Monte Carlo engine with historical crisis replays (2008 GFC, 1970s stagflation, 2000 tech crash), Annual | Quarterly | Monthly historical net worth granularity, and one-click 30-Year Forecast CSV Export.

Company Equity (RSUs, ISOs, NSOs & Buybacks)

Models multi-tranche RSU vesting schedules, ISO/NSO option strike spreads, and AMT preference adjustments (IRC § 56(b)(3)). In Longevity & Scenarios, company equity events support four exercise and exit strategies across single-year or staged multi-year tranches: Cashless Repurchase (Exercise & Sell All), Exercise & Hold with Cash (pay strike and NSO ordinary income tax from liquid cash reserves), Exercise & Hold with Sell-to-Cover (sell enough options to cover strike and NSO tax), and Sell Held Shares Later (a two-stage plan where you exercise and hold options in one year to start the long-term capital gains clock, then sell held shares in a later year or tranche taxed at long-term capital gains, NIIT, and state capital gains rates above the stepped-up cost basis). Each scenario also supports a custom Equity Growth Rate (%) override in Edit Plan, and Editing any shared life event isolates it to the active scenario so sibling plans remain untouched.

Education & 529 Depletion Waterfall

Tracks K–12, undergraduate, and graduate tuition schedules per child. Applies a strict custodial drawdown waterfall that depletes each student's tax-free IRC § 529 account first before drawing remaining tuition from parent taxable brokerage cash flows, and evaluates SECURE 2.0 Act § 126 Roth IRA rollovers for unused funds.

Estate Taxes & Irrevocable Trusts

Computes federal estate tax exposure (including TCJA exemption sunset toggles) alongside state-specific estate tax brackets and cliff rules (WA, NY, MA, OR, etc.). Simulates transferring appreciating assets into SLATs, IDGTs (with IRC § 671 grantor tax burn), GRATs, and ILITs.

Multi-State Tax Arbitrage

Compares net take-home cash flow and 10-year compounding deltas across Washington, California, New York, Texas, and Florida across W-2 salary, RSU/option vesting, long-term capital gains (including WA RCW 82.87 excise tax), and portfolio yield.

Investments, Philanthropy & Form 709 Gifting

Audits portfolio asset allocation against target IPS presets (Three-Fund Boglehead, Endowment, Cash-Flow Barbelled), models Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) appreciated-stock bunching vs. standard deductions, and tracks annual IRC § 2503(b) gift exclusions per recipient.

Quarterly RSU Supplemental Withholding Gap

Employers typically withhold federal tax on RSU vests at the flat statutory supplemental rate (22% up to the statutory threshold, 37% above) rather than your true marginal bracket. The Compensation & Taxes engine calculates your exact quarterly under-withholding gap across Q1–Q4 (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15) so you can adjust W-4 Line 4(c) or make estimated payments.
