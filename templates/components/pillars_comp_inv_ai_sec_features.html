Audits multi-account portfolio allocations against Investment Policy Statement (IPS) targets and optimizes asset placement across taxable, tax-deferred, and tax-free accounts.

Tax Location Optimization & Drift Monitoring

Holding the right asset in the wrong account type creates cumulative tax drag over decades. The portfolio engine syncs live account balances and holdings via SimpleFIN Bridge (direct read-only token access to 16,000+ North American financial institutions), Monarch Money (GraphQL account, holding, and historical balance snapshot sync), Manual Account Entry, or CSV Balance & History Import/Export. All provider credentials, setup tokens, and account histories are encrypted at rest with the household's AES-256-GCM key alongside historical IRS Form 1040 transcript records to evaluate tax location efficiency and rebalancing drift across all accounts.

  • Asset Location Hierarchy: Directs tax-inefficient assets (fixed income, REITs) into tax-deferred accounts (Traditional 401(k)/IRA), highest expected growth into tax-free accounts (Roth IRA, HSA), and broad index equities into taxable brokerage.
  • Core & Satellite Allocation: Benchmarks total household exposure against target IPS weights (e.g. 50% US Total Market, 20% International Developed/Emerging, 15% US Aggregate Bonds, 5% Cash, 10% Satellite Growth).
  • ±5% Drift Tolerance Rebalancing: Monitors allocation drift and directs rebalancing through incoming cash flows, dividend sweeps, and tax-free retirement reallocations rather than realizing taxable gains in brokerage accounts.
  • Private LP Commitment Book & Core Burn Calibration: Track private equity, venture, and real estate LP commitments (Committed, Called, Unfunded, DPI/RVPI/TVPI, mark staleness, and Schedule K-1 status), stress-test 18-month accelerated capital calls in scenario cash flows, and calibrate core living burn from trailing 12-month Monarch cash flows.

Privacy & Security

Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Encryption

Personal finances should stay strictly private. The project uses zero-knowledge encryption so that not even the creator or server administrators can view your data.

Zero-Knowledge Vault Encryption

Your data is encrypted using a private key stored only in your Google Drive (or browser cookie in Zero-Retention Sandbox). Not even our team, developers, or server administrators can decrypt or view your finances unless you explicitly turn on support access.

Encrypted Provider Credentials & Tokens

Whether you connect via SimpleFIN Bridge access URLs or Monarch Money session credentials, all provider tokens and passwords are encrypted at rest with your private household key—never stored in plaintext, and never accessible to administrators when support access is locked.

Zero Tracking & No Data Selling

No advertising trackers, no data brokers or reselling, and no training of commercial AI models on your personal financial history.
