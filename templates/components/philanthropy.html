Tax Strategy: Appreciated Asset Donation vs. Cash Donation

Donating long-term appreciated equities or index fund shares directly to a Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) or public charity permanently eliminates accrued capital gains taxes (up to 20% federal + 3.8% NIIT + applicable state capital gains) while capturing a full fair-market-value itemized tax deduction (up to 37% federal ordinary bracket). For high-bracket households, every dollar gifted directly in low-basis stock shields significant wealth compared to selling shares and donating cash.
