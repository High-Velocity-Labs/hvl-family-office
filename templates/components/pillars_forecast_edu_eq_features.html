Test Individual Life Decisions Without Duplicating Spreadsheets

Keep one Baseline Plan connected to your live accounts and cash flows, then spin up lightweight What-If Plans to evaluate specific choices—like college options, home purchases, or early retirement—side-by-side.

  • Isolated What-If Plans: Test "Private College" vs. "In-State Public" or "Retire at 50" vs. "One More Year" without altering your baseline numbers.
  • Side-by-Side Comparison Scorecard & Drag-and-Drop Reordering: Compare liquid wealth at age 60, retirement readiness age, and cumulative tax drag across plans with two-way HTML5 drag handles across pills and comparison rows.
  • Non-Destructive Scenario Archiving: Archive obsolete or exploratory plans to keep the comparison scorecard focused without losing historical assumptions, with a 1-click Show / Hide Archived toggle.
  • Automatic Baseline Updates: When your real-world account balances, savings, or compensation change, all of your what-if scenarios stay in sync automatically.
  • Multi-Granularity History & 30-Year Forecast CSV Export: Toggle historical net worth views between Annual, Quarterly (20Q), and Monthly (36M) intervals with automatic gap interpolation, and export the full multi-decade projection table to CSV.
  • Dynamic Spending Guardrails, Bracket-Fill Roth Ladders & Drawdown Order: Configure per-plan drawdown ordering (Taxable → Pre-Tax → Roth, Pre-Tax First, or Pro-Rata), dynamic Federal bracket-fill Roth conversions (auto-filling the 22% or 24% bracket), bear-market spending guardrails, and one-click Pre-65 ACA Healthcare Bridge and IRS Rule 72(t) SEPP distributions via Scenario Playbooks (/playbooks).

Pillar 2: Education Modeling

Tuition Ledger Integration & 529 Outflow Modeling

Connects multi-year school tuition schedules directly to long-term forecast cash flows, modeling tax-free 529 depletion and parental liquidity impact.

Dual-Mode Education Events

Education milestones support two operational modes: syncing directly with recorded tuition schedules, or overriding values to test hypothetical college scenarios.

  • Synced with Ledger: Pulls grade progression, enrolled tuition rates, and graduation years directly from your multi-year education schedule.
  • Automatic 529 Custodial Depletion: Tuition draws tax-free from the designated child's 529 reserve first. Only after the 529 is exhausted does the engine draw shortfall cash from parents' liquid brokerage.
  • SECURE 2.0 § 126 Rollover Planning: Details statutory rules for rolling up to $35,000 of leftover 529 funds per beneficiary tax-free into personal Roth IRAs.
