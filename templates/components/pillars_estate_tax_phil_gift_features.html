Pillar 6: Philanthropic Giving

Donor-Advised Funds & Appreciated Stock Tax Optimization

Maximizes philanthropic impact while eliminating capital gains taxes through structured DAF grants, appreciated equity donations, and multi-year budget reconciliation.

Appreciated Stock Donation vs. Cash Donation

Directly gifting appreciated equities, stock options, or index fund shares held over one year to a 501(c)(3) public charity or Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) delivers a dual tax shield exceeding standard cash gifts.

  • Permanent Capital Gains Tax Elimination: Eradicates 100% of accrued federal long-term capital gains tax (20%), Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT 3.8%), and applicable state capital gains on donated shares.
  • Full Fair-Market-Value Itemized Deduction: Captures an immediate itemized deduction for the full fair market value (FMV) up to 30% of AGI, shielding top-bracket federal ordinary income (up to 37%).
  • Multi-Year Grants Ledger: Tracks historical and forward-planned charitable distributions, reconciling annual budgets against confirmed allocations.
  • Recipient Organization Analytics: Segment annual giving by category across education, medical research, community foundations, and environmental causes.
