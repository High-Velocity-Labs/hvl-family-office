Every mode runs the exact same deterministic Python forecasting, equity, estate, and statutory tax engines. Choose the workflow that matches how you prefer to store your household vault:

Mode A: Cloud Vault (Sign in with Google)

  1. Visit /login and click Sign in with Google under Cloud Vault Sign In.
  2. Envelope-Encrypted Vault Key Storage: An isolated encrypted SQLite household vault is provisioned for you, and your Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) decryption key is stored in your personal Google Drive (appDataFolder) and browser cookie—never retained in plaintext on the server.

Mode B: Anonymous Workspace (No Login Required)

  1. Click Launch Anonymous Workspace (/sandbox)—no account, email, or login required.
  2. 24h / 7d Server Auto-Wipe: Your workspace is encrypted with a browser-only key and automatically wiped from the server after 24 hours (or click Save for 7 Days in the top bar to extend).

2. Connecting Financial Accounts SimpleFIN · Monarch · Manual · CSV

In Cloud Vault, Anonymous Workspace, and Desktop modes alike, you can populate your household balance sheet, investment holdings, historical net worth series, and cash flows using any combination of these four methods:

Option A: SimpleFIN Bridge (Direct Bank Sync)

SimpleFIN connects directly to 16,000+ North American banks and brokerages via read-only tokens without sharing your banking passwords with this application:

  1. Create a SimpleFIN Bridge account at beta-bridge.simplefin.org and link your financial institutions.
  2. Generate a single-use Base64 Setup Token in SimpleFIN.
  3. In HVL Family Office, open Settings → Account Connections (or Step 3 of the Guided Setup Wizard), paste the Setup Token, and click Connect SimpleFIN.
  4. The application exchanges your Setup Token once for an encrypted Access URL, syncs current balances and holdings, and reconstructs up to 90 days of daily historical balances from posted transactions.

Option B: Monarch Money Integration

If you aggregate your household accounts in Monarch Money:

  1. Open Settings → Account Connections.
  2. Sign in with your Monarch email, password, and MFA code (or paste an existing Monarch session token).
  3. Click Sync Now in the sidebar anytime to pull updated account balances, individual ticker holdings, historical account balance snapshots, and spending/cash-flow averages via GraphQL.

Option C: Manual Entry & Asset Roles

  • Direct Account & Holding Entry: Add accounts, holdings, equity grants, and real estate directly in the Accounts and Company Equity tabs—no external aggregator required.
  • Assigning Account Roles: In Settings → Account Roles, classify each account (Taxable Brokerage, Pre-Tax 401k/IRA, Roth IRA, 529 College, Primary Residence, HELOC/Debt, or Hidden) to assign its statutory tax treatment for forecasting, Monte Carlo simulations, and IRS withdrawal waterfalls.
  • Dated Balance Logging: Record dated balance snapshots or edit historical points directly on any account. Toggle the Overview Net Worth chart between Annual, Quarterly (20Q), and Monthly (36M) views with linear or carry-forward interpolation across missing dates.

Option D: CSV Balance History Import & Export

  • Historical CSV Import: Upload historical balance series in Accounts, Settings, or Step 3 of the Guided Setup Wizard using either standard row format (Date, Account, Balance exported from Monarch or spreadsheets) or multi-column date-by-account tables.
  • Sample Template Download: Download the pre-formatted CSV template directly from Step 3 of the Guided Setup Wizard or the Accounts tab to populate your historical timeline in seconds.
  • Download Your Data (CSV Export): Export your complete accounts inventory and dated balance history to CSV at any time for external backup, spreadsheet modeling, or migration.

3. IRS Tax Transcripts, W-2 PDFs & Withholding In-Memory PDF Parsing

Populate multi-year tax history, W-2 compensation baselines, and Safe Harbor withholding thresholds.

Which IRS Transcript to Download

  1. Sign in to your individual account at irs.gov/individuals/get-transcript.
  2. Download the Record of Account Transcript or Return Transcript PDF for each tax year (these contain your Adjusted Gross Income, Taxable Income, Total Tax, and Withholding lines).
  3. Upload the PDFs in Compensation & Taxes → IRS Transcripts.

Form W-2 Upload & Safe Harbor

  • Upload employer Form W-2 PDFs in Compensation & Taxes to extract Box 1 wages, Box 2 federal withholding, Box 12 retirement deferrals, Box 14 RSU/other items, and Box 16/17 state wages/tax.
  • Zero Disk Persistence: Uploaded W-2 and IRS Transcript PDFs are parsed strictly in RAM and immediately discarded—raw PDF files are never saved to disk.
  • Safe Harbor Penalty Protection: Compares your year-to-date withholding against the statutory 100% / 110% of Prior-Year Tax and 90% of Current-Year Tax thresholds.

4. AI Financial Advisor & BYO Gemini API Key Google Gemini API

The built-in AI Financial Advisor queries your household's encrypted records and runs deterministic calculations before answering, with support for an optional Bring-Your-Own (BYO) Google Gemini API key.

Shared Server Key vs. BYO Key Rate Limits

  • Cloud Vault & Anonymous Workspace — Shared Server Key (Default): Works immediately out of the box with quota guardrails (5/min, 30/hr, and 2,000 characters per prompt) to protect shared API capacity.
  • BYO Gemini Key (Optional Override): Supplying your own free Google AI Studio key in Settings → Account Connections isolates your household from shared quota and unlocks higher limits (30/min, 300/hr, and 8,000 characters per prompt).
  • Personal Assistant MCP Gateway (POST /mcp): Mint a token-wrapped bearer token under Settings → Integrations to connect external MCP clients directly to your 20 non-mutating household read and simulation tools without consuming server-side Gemini API quota.
  • Standalone Desktop App (LOCAL_MODE): Local desktop installs always run with the full 30/min · 300/hr · 8,000 char ceiling using your own key.

How to Configure Your Free Gemini API Key

  1. Visit Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey) and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy the key string (starts with AIza...).
  3. In HVL Family Office, open Settings → Account Connections, paste your key, and click Save Key.
  4. Your key is encrypted with your household's Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) key and stored only inside your vault.

Model Tier Selection & Performance

Within the AI Financial Advisor chat drawer, click the model selector in the top toolbar to switch between available models:

  • Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite (Default): Lowest latency, highest daily quota headroom, ideal for everyday scenario lookups, net worth summaries, and tax questions.
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash: Balanced multi-tool reasoning across complex multi-step scenario modifications.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro: Maximum depth for comprehensive multi-decade tax and estate structuring.
  • Gemma 2 9B: Local or lightweight tool-free conversational fallback.
