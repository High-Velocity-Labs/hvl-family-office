 Surfaces actionable probate, beneficiary, liquidity, and tax-withholding gaps directly from live household records and lets households sign off on items confirmed at their custodian.

 Connects illiquid LP capital call schedules and withdrawal sequencing directly into the 30-year scenario cash-flow and tax waterfall.
