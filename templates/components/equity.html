How to Read & Use the Valuation Sensitivity Curve

Purpose: Answers "If our company valuation reaches $X per share (or an IPO/strategic buyout occurs), how much cash do I actually take home?"
How to use it: Look at the green line (True Net Post-Tax Proceeds) at any hypothetical share price. It shows your take-home cash after deducting exercise strike costs and ordinary/capital gains taxes. Notice how higher valuations face state high-earner tax thresholds (such as Washington 9.9% SB 6346) once taxable option spread crosses $1M when simulated.
