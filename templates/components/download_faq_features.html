How Installation, Setup & In-Place Updates Work

The desktop app runs the exact same deterministic Python engines and encrypted SQLite vault as the web platform on 127.0.0.1:5050, opening directly in your default browser with zero cloud setup required.

  • 1. First-Launch Passphrase & Guided Onboarding: On first launch, choose an offline Master Passphrase or PIN (wrapping your 256-bit AES Fernet key via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations), restore an existing .fovault backup in Step 1 or configure your tax profile, link SimpleFIN Bridge / Monarch Money (or add accounts manually / import CSV balance history), and optionally add your own Google Gemini API Key in Settings → Account Connections.
  • 2. Portable .fovault Backups & Migration: In Step 1 of Guided Setup or Settings → Local Desktop Vault, export or restore a consistent .fovault encrypted database snapshot at any time to preserve snapshots or move between Cloud, Sandbox, and Local Desktop machines.
  • 3. Zero-Data-Loss App Updates: Because your vault lives in your OS user data folder outside the app binary, installing a new .dmg or Setup.exe never touches your data. When launched, the new version automatically stops any older background daemon, saves a timestamped pre_upgrade_vX.Y.Z.fovault snapshot in your backups/ folder, and applies schema migrations automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Objective answers to common questions regarding architecture, privacy, data synchronization, and financial modeling.

What is the difference between Cloud Vault Sign-In, Anonymous Workspace, Sample Households, and the Desktop App?

You can use HVL Family Office in four ways depending on your privacy and retention preference: (1) Cloud Vault Sign-In (/login)—sign in with Google OAuth to save your encrypted household vault across sessions with your encryption key stored in your personal Google Drive (note: hosted cloud access is a free, non-commercial hobby project with no retention SLA; hosting may end at any time and vaults inactive for 365+ days may be deleted, so export a .fovault backup anytime); (2) Anonymous Workspace (/sandbox)—no login required, encrypted with a browser-only key that auto-wipes from the server after 24 hours (or 7 days), plus you can save/open a local .fovault file on your computer to keep your work permanently even if you clear browser cache; (3) Read-Only Sample Households to browse 6 pre-built demo families; or (4) Standalone Desktop App / Docker (macOS & Windows) to run 100% locally on your own machine forever.

Do I need a Monarch Money subscription, and how does this differ from Empower or Excel?

No Monarch subscription is required. You can connect banks and brokerages directly via SimpleFIN Bridge, link an existing Monarch Money account, enter accounts and dated balances manually, or import/export CSV balance histories. HVL Family Office is built to complement day-to-day trackers by focusing on forward-looking modeling—branching scenario trees, private company equity (AMT, 83(b), QSBS), state estate tax calculations, and IRC § 6654 safe harbor payroll withholding.

How does account, historical balance, and tax sync work?

Live balances and holdings sync via SimpleFIN Bridge (read-only Access URL token) or Monarch Money (GraphQL), or can be entered manually and imported/exported via CSV. Historical net worth timelines automatically fill gaps using Monarch snapshot backfills, SimpleFIN 90-day transaction walk-back reconstruction, and carry-forward interpolation across dated balance entries. All provider tokens, credentials, and in-memory parsed IRS Form 1040 / W-2 records are encrypted at rest with your household key.

Can platform administrators or creators see my data?

No. Financial records are encrypted using a private key stored only in your Google Drive (when signed in with Google OAuth), in your browser cookie only (in Anonymous Workspace, with zero server key retention), or wrapped by your local PIN / vault key on desktop. Not even our developers or server administrators can decrypt or view your finances.

How does the AI Advisor avoid hallucinations, stay on-topic & handle API quotas?

The conversational advisor is strictly scoped to financial, tax, equity, and estate topics—refusing off-topic requests—and queries deterministic calculation engines (equity vesting schedules, statutory tax engines, and Monte Carlo simulations) before answering. In Cloud Vault & Anonymous Workspace modes, visitors can start immediately on the shared server key (5/min, 30/hr, 2,000 chars/prompt), or supply their own free Google AI Studio (BYO Gemini API key) in Settings to unlock higher limits (30/min, 300/hr, 8,000 chars/prompt) on an isolated quota. Standalone desktop installs always run directly against your own key.

How do I migrate from an Anonymous Workspace to a Cloud Vault or the Desktop App?

There are two ways to carry your work forward without re-entering data: (1) Direct In-Session Cloud Vault Upgrade—while inside an active Anonymous Workspace, click Save to Cloud Vault in the top workspace banner (or inside Settings → Household & Access) and sign in with Google; your accounts, scenarios, and tax settings are automatically migrated, re-encrypted with your Google Drive key, and the temporary workspace is shredded; or (2) Portable .fovault File Transfer—click Download .fovault in your Anonymous Workspace to save an encrypted snapshot to your computer, then upload it on Step 1 of the Guided Setup Wizard or in Settings → Household & Access inside your Cloud Vault, a future Anonymous Workspace, or the offline Desktop App.

How was this built, and can I inspect or self-host the code?

HVL Family Office was architected and built at High Velocity Labs using human-directed, multi-agent AI software engineering paired with strict deterministic guardrails. Every release is gated by an automated adversarial security audit that inspects raw SQLite ciphertext on disk, enforces parameterized SQL and XSS escaping, and runs 200+ statutory tax, RSU, and Monte Carlo unit tests. Source code, Docker containers, and SLSA-attested macOS/Windows desktop builds are available for noncommercial personal use on GitHub.
