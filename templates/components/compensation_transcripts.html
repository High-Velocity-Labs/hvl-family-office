Ephemeral In-Memory Processing: Uploaded W-2 and transcript PDFs are parsed via RAM streams (io.BytesIO). Zero PDF files are ever stored on the server disk. Data is encrypted directly into the AES-128 database.
Where to get your official IRS Transcript PDFs:
  1. Go to IRS.gov/individuals/get-transcript (or log in directly to your IRS Online Account).
  2. Sign in securely with ID.me or your IRS credentials.
  3. Select Federal Tax (or Other) as the reason for requesting your transcript.
  4. Under the Wage & Income Transcript box (or Tax Return Transcript for full Form 1040), click on the specific tax year (e.g. 2025, 2024, 2023).
  5. Download the official PDF generated in your browser, select the taxpayer below, and click Analyze Transcript PDF (or upload directly on the Career Earnings subtab above).
* The IRS Wage & Income Transcript compiles all Form W-2s, 1099s, and federal withholdings submitted by employers directly to the IRS. Standard employer Form W-2 PDFs are also fully supported.
