ProjectionLab × HVL Family Office: Building Plans from Scratch vs. Easy Side-by-Side "What-If" Comparison

ProjectionLab and HVL Family Office both exist because traditional expense trackers (like Monarch Money or Copilot) look backward at last month's transactions rather than forward across a 30-year life plan. While ProjectionLab is a commercial SaaS product and HVL Family Office is 100% free and open source (GitHub), they also take very different architectural approaches to how easily you can explore and compare "what-if" life decisions side-by-side and how much manual setup work is required.

🛠️ ProjectionLab's Core Lens (Commercial SaaS): Bottom-Up "Financial CAD" Studio

ProjectionLab is built like a deterministic CAD studio for DIY planners. You start from a blank canvas and wire every account, income stream, expense phase, and priority rule by hand. That bottom-up rules engine gives power users deep control (Sankey charts, 150-year historical backtesting, 72(t) SEPP), with the trade-off that initial setup is heavy and testing multiple "what-if" plans requires cloning standalone copies that must be updated manually whenever your baseline changes.

📈 HVL's Core Lens (Free & Open Source): Easy Side-by-Side "What-If" Scenario Planning

HVL Family Office is a free, open-source project built around exploring and comparing competing "what-if" life scenarios side-by-side in seconds. Your baseline plan stays anchored to your live data (Monarch/SimpleFIN sync, W-2/IRS PDFs, equity grants, mortgage payoff, and kids' college costs), and you can spin up lightweight "what-if" plans — toggling life events like Early FIRE + Pre-65 ACA Healthcare Bridge, Home Tear-Down/Rebuild, Moving States, or Option Exercises on and off — while comparing every plan head-to-head across a 98-cohort (1928–2025) backtester, single-year Sankey cash flows, and scorecard. Why Households Hit the "Monarch + ProjectionLab + Claude" Wall

Consider a classic FIRE question: "What is our steady-state spending excluding kids, our mortgage (once paid off), and a one-off home tear-down/rebuild — and can we step off a high W-2?"

The Workflow Gap

1. Where Monarch Money Stops

Monarch is great at aggregating transactions and categorizing historical cash flow, but its data model is observational. Its built-in AI assistant can summarize what you spent on dining last quarter, yet it has no multi-decade 3-pool balance sheet simulator, no statutory tax/AMT/IRMAA engine, and no structural separation between core baseline burn and temporary life layers (kids' K–12/529 outflows, amortizing mortgage P&I, or one-off capital projects).

2. Why Raw Claude / ChatGPT Hallucinates

Exporting Monarch CSVs into a frontier LLM gives you a fluent conversation, but raw language models do not execute deterministic multi-year tax brackets, ISO AMT crossovers, Roth conversion ladders, or 5,000-trial sequence-of-returns math in tokens. Worse, pasting unredacted brokerage statements and tax returns into a general chat window exposes sensitive household PII.

3. How HVL Bridges ProjectionLab & AI

Instead of choosing between ProjectionLab's manual setup or an ungrounded chatbot, HVL ingests your Monarch Money / SimpleFIN accounts and IRS/W-2 PDFs directly into deterministic Python engines — separating baseline living core from kid/education waterfalls, mortgage amortization, and one-off timeline pins — and exposes 13 live simulation tools to an integrated AI Modeling Assistant that strips PII before calling Gemini.

What Stands Out in ProjectionLab (Where ProjectionLab Excels)

Architectural and UX ideas that make ProjectionLab a favorite among DIY FIRE modelers — and where our philosophies overlap or diverge.

ProjectionLab's Standout Strengths

1. Historical Cohort Backtesting

Alongside randomized Monte Carlo trials, ProjectionLab replays retirement plans against historical starting cohorts — an approach HVL also supports via its 1928–2025 98-Cohort Historical Backtester (highlighting the 4 toughest historical starting years like 1929, 1966, 1973, and 2000).

2. Sankey Cash-Flow & Priority Waterfalls

ProjectionLab pairs interactive Sankey diagrams with a drag-and-drop cash-flow priority list for custom routing rules; HVL includes a Single-Year Cash-Flow Sankey inside its Cash Flow lens coupled with an automated 3-pool tax-efficient drawdown waterfall.

3. ACA Subsidies & 72(t) SEPP Modeling

For early retirees bridging the gap before age 65 Medicare eligibility, ProjectionLab models MAGI-based ACA healthcare subsidies and Rule 72(t) SEPP; HVL models this via a dedicated Pre-65 Healthcare & ACA Subsidy Bridge event pin that auto-terminates at age 65.

4. International Tax Presets

While HVL is purpose-built for US Federal + 50-State statutory law, ProjectionLab supports tax presets and currency localization for households in Canada, the UK, Australia, the Netherlands, and Germany.

5. Certified Coach & Advisor Directory

Through ProjectionLab Pro and its public Advisor Directory, users who don't want to build a complex model alone can hire an hourly or flat-fee specialist to configure and audit their plan inside a shared workspace.

6. Plugin API for Custom Scripts

ProjectionLab's client-side Plugin API enables technical users to write local scripts or use community browser extensions to push balance updates from YNAB, Lunch Money, or Monarch without giving ProjectionLab bank credentials.

What HVL Family Office Focused On (Why We Built HVL Differently)

Where HVL went deep to make "what-if" comparisons effortless, eliminate manual setup work, and pair deterministic math with conversational AI.

HVL's Core Differentiators

1. Easy Side-by-Side "What-If" Comparison

Compare competing life plans head-to-head (e.g., Baseline vs. Early FIRE at 50 vs. Home Rebuild + Sabbatical) by simply toggling life-event pins on and off — and unlike cloned plans in ProjectionLab, every child plan stays automatically synced whenever your baseline updates (explore Playbooks →).

2. Tool-Calling AI Over 13 Live Engines

You can ask the AI Modeling Assistant directly: "What is our steady-state FIRE spending once the kids graduate and the mortgage is paid off, and what happens if we add a home rebuild in 2028?" The assistant invokes deterministic Python tools to compute the exact answer rather than guessing in tokens.

3. Native Monarch + SimpleFIN + PDF Ingestion

No community browser extensions or 20-hour manual setup required: connect Monarch Money or SimpleFIN directly, and drop in W-2s and IRS Tax Return/Wage Transcripts to populate historical compensation, effective tax rates, and safe-harbor baselines in seconds.

4. Structural Separation of Kids, Mortgage & One-Offs

Instead of lumping all spending together or wiring dozens of manual expense rules, HVL's forecast engine natively isolates your Core Living Baseline from child-linked K–12 & 529 college waterfalls, mortgage payoff schedules, and toggleable timeline event pins.

5. Deep W-2 Equity, Safe-Harbor & Estate Math

Models multi-grant RSU quarterly vesting waterfalls, annual refresh grant stacking, statutory 22%/37% supplemental withholding shortfalls, ISO AMT crossovers, 50-state relocation tax arbitrage, Form 709 Crummey gifting ledgers, and appreciated-lot DAF deductions.

6. 100% Free, Open Source & Envelope-Encrypted Vault

Completely free and open-source on GitHub with zero paywalls: run in our Envelope-Encrypted Vault (with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) keys escrowed in your Google Drive appDataFolder), in our offline macOS/Windows Desktop App, or self-hosted via Docker with single-file .fovault portability.
