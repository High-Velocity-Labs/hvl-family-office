6. Desktop Installers, Local PIN Recovery & Self-Hosting macOS · Windows · Docker · Source

Instructions for running HVL Family Office 100% offline via the standalone desktop installers, managing your local vault files and PIN, or deploying via Docker/source.

macOS (.dmg) & Windows (.exe) Installers

  • macOS: Download HVL-Family-Office-macOS.dmg from GitHub Releases and drag HVL Family Office.app to /Applications. If macOS Gatekeeper prompts on first launch, right-click the app and select Open, or run: xattr -cr "/Applications/HVL Family Office.app".
  • Windows: Download and run HVL-Family-Office-Windows-Setup.exe from GitHub Releases (installs per-user into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\HVLFamilyOffice; no Admin rights required).
  • Zero-Data-Loss Updates: Installing a newer .dmg or .exe never overwrites your vault data; on launch, it automatically backs up backups/pre_upgrade_*.fovault and migrates your schema in-place.

Where Local Desktop Vault Files Live on Disk

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/HVLFamilyOffice/
  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\HVLFamilyOffice\
  • Linux / Docker: ~/.local/share/hvl-family-office/ (or /app/data)
  • Contains global.db, families/1.db, .mm/.secret.key (44-char local recovery key), and backups/. Click Open Data Folder in Settings to view in Finder/Explorer.

Local Desktop PIN & Recovery Key

  • First-Run PIN: On first desktop launch, create a local PIN or Passphrase (4–128 chars, wrapped with scrypt and AES-256-GCM) and copy your 44-character Recovery Key in Settings → Household & Access.
  • Forgotten PIN Reset: Click "Forgot PIN or Passphrase?" on /login and paste the 44-character key from .mm/.secret.key inside your local vault folder (or sign in with Google if linked).

Building from Source & Docker

Compile your own native desktop installer or run via Docker:

pip install -r requirements.txt pyinstaller python scripts/build_desktop.py

Outputs are written to dist/HVL-Family-Office-macOS.dmg or dist/HVL-Family-Office-Windows-Setup.exe.

7. Financial Modules & Methodology Reference All 11 Platform Engines

Inside the app, you can click "How It Works" in the section header on any page to open the contextual slide-out drawer for that module. Below is the complete reference across all 11 modules:

Forecasting, Scenario Trees & Forecast CSV Export

Simulates 53-year liquid net worth, cash flows, and retirement readiness ({{ current_year }}–{{ current_year + 52 }}). Social Security is estimated per person from each adult's current salary and bonus (capped at the wage base) and retirement age, or from the monthly benefit at full retirement age you enter from your ssa.gov statement in Settings, then adjusted for claim age; a spouse also receives the spousal top-up when it is higher. An adult with neither earnings nor an entered benefit gets no Social Security, and the forecast says so. Create a Baseline Parent Plan and branch lightweight Child Plans (e.g., early retirement, state relocation, private vs. public college) that inherit parent accounts while isolating decision overrides. Organize comparison plans using two-way HTML5 drag-and-drop reordering across selector pills and scorecard rows, and archive obsolete plans without deleting them via the Show / Hide Archived toggle. Includes a 1,000+ trial Monte Carlo engine with historical crisis replays (2008 GFC, 1970s stagflation, 2000 tech crash), Annual | Quarterly | Monthly historical net worth granularity, and one-click 53-Year Forecast CSV Export.

Company Equity (RSUs, ISOs, NSOs & Buybacks)

Models multi-tranche RSU vesting schedules, ISO/NSO option strike spreads (the year-by-year forecast taxes an ISO exercise like an NSO, with the spread as ordinary income plus Medicare, and carries no AMT; the equity analysis and advisor give an AMT estimate for the exercise; confirm ISO exercises with a tax professional). In Longevity & Scenarios, company equity events support four exercise and exit strategies across single-year or staged multi-year tranches: Cashless Repurchase (Exercise & Sell All), Exercise & Hold with Cash (pay strike and NSO ordinary income tax from liquid cash reserves), Exercise & Hold with Sell-to-Cover (sell enough options to cover strike and NSO tax), and Sell Held Shares Later (a two-stage plan where you exercise and hold options in one year to start the long-term capital gains clock, then sell held shares in a later year or tranche taxed on the gain above the exercise-date value, in the household's 0/15/20% long-term capital gains band plus the 3.8% NIIT above its threshold, and Washington's capital gains tax where applicable). Each scenario also supports a custom Equity Growth Rate (%) override in Edit Plan, and Editing any shared life event isolates it to the active scenario so sibling plans remain untouched.

Education & 529 Depletion Waterfall

Tracks K–12, undergraduate, and graduate tuition schedules per child. Applies a strict custodial drawdown waterfall that depletes each student's tax-free IRC § 529 account first before drawing remaining tuition from parent taxable brokerage cash flows, and shows the SECURE 2.0 Act § 126 Roth IRA rollover rules for unused funds as reference (the forecast does not project a rollover).

Estate Taxes & Irrevocable Trusts

Computes federal estate tax exposure (including a legislative-rollback stress test of the exemption) alongside state-specific estate tax brackets and cliff rules (WA, NY, MA, and OR). Simulates transferring appreciating assets into SLATs, IDGTs (modeled as a sale to the trust that removes an assumed 50% appreciation above the note from your estate), GRATs, and ILITs.

Multi-State Tax Arbitrage

Compares state tax and recurring costs (property tax, cost-of-living-adjusted spending, sales tax) with 1-year and 5-year totals across Washington (current law and with the 9.9% tax), California, New York, Oregon, Nevada, Texas, Florida, and Wyoming (plus your home state) for W-2 salary, option spread, and long-term capital gains (including WA RCW 82.87 excise tax). State income tax uses one flat top rate per state; there is no yield input.

Investments, Philanthropy & Form 709 Gifting

Audits portfolio asset allocation against target IPS presets (Three-Fund Boglehead, Endowment, Cash-Flow Barbelled), models Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) appreciated-stock gifts with a flat 35% federal deduction offset (it does not compare itemizing against the standard deduction), and tracks annual IRC § 2503(b) gift exclusions per recipient.

RSU Supplemental Withholding Gap

Employers typically withhold federal tax on RSU vests at the flat statutory supplemental rate (22% up to the statutory threshold, 37% above) rather than your true marginal bracket. The Compensation & Taxes engine compares your year-to-date federal withholding and the number of paychecks remaining this year (you enter it; there is no default) against the safe-harbor target to estimate the remaining shortfall, so you can adjust W-4 Line 4(c) or make estimated payments.
