SECURE 2.0 § 126 Rollover & Legacy Strategy

When 529 education custodial accounts are fully funded, up to $35,000 of leftover 529 funds per beneficiary can be rolled over tax-free and penalty-free into the beneficiary's Roth IRA under IRC § 529(c)(3)(E) (SECURE 2.0 Act § 126), if the 529 account has been open for at least 15 years, contributions (and their earnings) from the last 5 years are excluded, each year's rollover stays within that year's annual IRA contribution limit, and the beneficiary has earned income at least equal to the rollover. The $35,000 is a lifetime cap. Remaining funds can pay for graduate/medical/law schools, or be changed to another family beneficiary (a transfer to someone in a lower generation can be a taxable gift or generation-skipping transfer).
