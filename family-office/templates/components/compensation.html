Why the Prior-Year Safe Harbor Matters for Company Stock

Under IRC § 6654(d)(1)(C), taxpayers whose prior year AGI exceeded $150,000 ($75,000 if married filing separately) can immunize themselves from IRS underpayment penalties (Form 2210) by withholding 110% of prior-year total tax (100% below that AGI) ({{ (ssr_compensation and ssr_compensation.sh_rule_target) or '--' }}).

Even if you exercise options and generate millions in additional taxable income late in the year, meeting this threshold eliminates all estimated tax penalties.
